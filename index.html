How it works

  1. Film a short clip

    Record 30 to 60 seconds of the moments that worry you, at home, at your own pace. Tell us what happened before and how often it happens.

  2. A therapist watches

    A qualified occupational therapist reviews your clip and writes back within 72 hours: what they saw, what it might mean, and what to do next.

  3. Follow your roadmap

    See exactly where you are on the road to an assessment, NHS or private, with guides and at-home activities for every step.

Little Signals does not diagnose autism. Our therapists share observations from short videos to help you understand what you're seeing and talk to professionals. Only a qualified assessment team can make a diagnosis. If you're worried about your child's health right now, contact your GP or call NHS 111. In an emergency, call 999.
