Common questions

Is this a diagnosis?

No. Our occupational therapists describe what they can see in your clips and suggest next steps. Only a qualified assessment team can diagnose autism, and our report is designed to help you talk to them.

How quickly will I hear back?

Within 72 hours of sending your clips. We'll email you (and notify you in the app) as soon as your feedback is ready.

What should I film?

Everyday moments that worry you, as they happen. Short clips of 5 to 10 seconds work well, ideally with your child's face and hands in view. You can send several clips up to the length of your plan.

Who sees my child's videos?

Only the occupational therapist assigned to your review, through private links that expire after 15 minutes. Clips are deleted 90 days after your feedback is sent, and we never use them for advertising or to train AI. See our privacy policy.

Do I need the iPhone app?

No. You can do everything here on our website, on a computer or any phone. The iPhone app is coming soon, and your account works on both.

Can I cancel the Journey plan?

Yes, any time. The first 2 months are paid together when you join; after that you can cancel whenever you like and you won't be charged again.
